# Perfect Studio – React Web Application

A fully responsive, editorial-grade React web application faithfully recreating the design, legacy, and interactive user experience of **Perfect Studio** (Chennai, Tamil Nadu – Since 1962).

---

## 📸 Key Highlights & Design Fidelity

- **Heritage Branding**: Faithful recreation of the 60+ year studio brand founded in 1962, covering locations in **Anna Nagar** (3rd Main Rd) and **Mogappair**, Chennai.
- **Color Palette & Visual Hierarchy**:
  - Heritage Burgundy: `#690000`, Deep Burgundy `#470000`, Strip Red `#8b0000`
  - Warm Sand & Gold: `#c8a95e`, `#f8e2a5`, `#ffe4b8`
  - Dark Editorial Tone: `#030303`, `#110201`, `#020202`
  - Paper Cream Backgrounds: `#fffdfb`, `#fffdf8`, `#f7f3ef`
- **Typography**:
  - Headings: `Cormorant Garamond` & `Playfair Display`
  - Body & UI: `Open Sans`
- **Micro-Interactions**: Light sweep button animations (`.btn::before`), floating contact rail, auto-scrolling photo marquee tracks, interactive before-and-after photo restoration slider, and image lightbox.

---

## 🌟 15+ Pages Implemented

1. **Home (`/`)**: Hero, statistics bar, legacy intro, 8 photography services, filterable portfolio with lightbox & marquee, portrait studio highlight, why choose us (5 features), customer testimonials carousel, booking CTA, accordion FAQ, footer.
2. **About Us (`/about`)**: Six decades of history since 1962, in-house colour lab philosophy, craftsmanship split-block, legacy timeline, and walk-in invitations.
3. **Services (`/services`)**: Comprehensive overview of all 8 photography categories with feature lists, starting prices, and direct links.
4. **Wedding Photography (`/services/wedding-photography`)**: Muhurtham rituals, candid coverage, cinematic films, and Italian leather album craft.
5. **Pre-Wedding Photography (`/services/pre-wedding-photography`)**: Outdoor ECR & Mahabalipuram couple shoots, concept moodboards, and teasers.
6. **Engagement Photography (`/services/engagement-photography`)**: Nichayathartham, ring exchange, and family group portraits.
7. **Candid Photography (`/services/candid-photography`)**: Unscripted, documentary photojournalism with natural light mastery.
8. **Maternity Photography (`/services/maternity-photography`)**: Motherhood sessions, gowns, studio/outdoor settings.
9. **Baby & Birthday Photography (`/services/baby-birthday-photography`)**: Newborn safety, first-year milestones, and cake smash themes.
10. **Corporate Photography (`/services/corporate-photography`)**: Tech summits, conferences, brand activations, and executive headshots.
11. **Portrait Photography (`/services/portrait-photography`)**: Anna Nagar & Mogappair studio spaces, 3-generation family portraits, and artistic headshots.
12. **Photo Restoration (`/services/photo-restoration`)**: Dedicated vintage photo repair lab with interactive before/after slider and 4-step archival workflow.
13. **Blog (`/blog`)**: Articles on choosing wedding photographers, Chennai wedding trends, and heirloom photo preservation with search and category filters.
14. **Blog Post Reader (`/blog/:id`)**: Full-length editorial articles with author bios, read times, and consultation actions.
15. **Contact Us (`/contact`)**: Dual studio cards (Anna Nagar & Mogappair), phone numbers, working hours, Google Maps links, and interactive contact form.
16. **Book Now (`/book-now`)**: Comprehensive booking consultation form with validation, preferred studio selector, and celebratory feedback.
